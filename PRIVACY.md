# Privacy

email-agent is a self-hosted personal tool. It runs on the owner's own machine and reads and sends
mail only for the Google account that authorized it. Message contents are sent to the configured
LLM provider to draft replies and to the configured SMS provider to reach the owner's phone. No data
is shared with anyone else, stored on third-party servers by this project, or used for any other
purpose. Revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
